# Money Account Selection Report

## Selected Platform: Coinbase

### Recommendation
We recommend **Coinbase** as our primary platform for the Money company operational wallet and treasury.

### Rationale
1. **User Preference:** Coinbase is explicitly the user's preferred platform.
2. **Isolated Subaccounts:** Coinbase supports creating a dedicated subaccount with isolated API keys and full transfer capabilities, ensuring complete separation from personal assets while giving the agent full custody and programmatic control.
3. **Operational Efficiency:** Native APIs for Coinbase Advanced/Pro provide robust programmatic order execution, balance checking, and transfer capabilities without the high gas fee volatility and complex smart contract interactions associated with some DeFi setups at our initial zero-investment scale.
4. **Security & Compliance:** Institutional-grade custody standards provide peace of mind while maintaining automated execution paths.
